# Wizzzee 0.3.0

Wizzzee is a disk space analyzer for macOS in the shape of [WizTree](https://diskanalyzer.com/): a folder tree with sizes, a list of the biggest files, and a colored treemap you can click into. It scans a full startup disk — around 3.9 million files — in roughly 15 seconds.

**Upgrade to this one.** On 0.2.x, the right-click menu would delete the entire folder you had just scanned — including your home folder or a whole volume — and the confirmation dialog told you it was about to. It is refused outright now.

## Wizzzee could delete the folder you scanned

When a scan finishes, its top row — the folder or volume you scanned — is **selected already**. Right-clicking it offered "Move to Trash" and "Delete Permanently…" like any other row, and nothing stopped either one.

On a scan of your home folder, the confirmation read:

> Permanently delete "/Users/you" and 412,309 items inside it?

Confirming did exactly that. On a whole volume it removed everything macOS's own System Integrity Protection didn't cover — your applications, your home folder, anything mounted under `/Volumes` — before finally erroring out on the parts it couldn't touch.

Wizzzee now refuses to remove a volume, any home folder, or the folder a scan was rooted at. The refusal is enforced in three separate places, and the menu no longer offers the actions at all — it explains why instead.

If you used the right-click menu on a scan's top row in an earlier version, that is what it did.

## The delete confirmation quoted the wrong amount

The figure in "… will be reclaimed. This bypasses the Trash and cannot be undone" was measured with the wrong ruler.

- **Sparse files were quoted at their full logical size.** Wizzzee measures space actually on disk by default — the whole point being reclaimable space — but this one number ignored that. A 200 GB disk image that occupies 8 GB promised 200 GB back from an irreversible delete.
- **Hard links were quoted at full size.** Deleting one name of a file that has several frees nothing at all while another name still exists. Wizzzee now counts those as freeing nothing, and says so, rather than quoting a number your disk will disagree with afterwards.

## Removing a big folder no longer freezes the app

Deleting a large folder ran on the main thread, so the entire window locked up for as long as it took — minutes for something like a big `node_modules` or an Xcode `DerivedData` — with no spinner, no progress and no way to stop it. Long enough, in the worst case, that macOS could decide the app had hung and terminate it part-way through, leaving a half-removed folder and totals that never updated.

It now runs in the background with a progress bar and a Stop button in the status bar. Stop takes effect at the next item — removing a single folder is one uninterruptible operation, so it can't be halted mid-way through one.

## Scans of `/System/Volumes/Data` couldn't delete anything

That path is the mount point of your Mac's writable data volume, and a perfectly reasonable thing to scan directly. Every delete in such a scan was refused with a message claiming your own home folder is on the sealed, read-only system volume. It isn't.

## Folders that couldn't be fully read said so

If reading a folder failed part-way through, Wizzzee kept whatever it had and reported the folder as completely scanned. A folder of 40,000 entries could contribute 3,000 of them to the total and look finished. Those are now marked **partly read** in the tree, so a number that is too low is visible rather than silent.

## Other fixes

- **Stopping a scan just as it finished threw the result away.** The completed scan is kept now.
- **Failed scans say what went wrong.** A missing folder and one you don't have permission to read produced the same "Couldn't scan" message. The permission case now names Full Disk Access, which is almost always the cause.
- **Double-clicking a small folder tile in the treemap** zoomed to its parent instead of into it — and did nothing at all when that parent was already the view. It was the only way to reach folders past the map's depth limit.
- **The treemap's hover outline** no longer lingers over the wrong tile after the pane is resized.
- **Rescan** is greyed out during a scan instead of being offered and silently doing nothing.
- **The file-type legend and the File View filter** no longer redo, on every redraw and every keystroke, sorting and allocation whose answer cannot have changed.

## Internal

This release came out of a full code review of the app; the write-up, including the three findings that turned out to be wrong on inspection, is in [`CODE_REVIEW.md`](https://github.com/SaiBarathR/wizzzee/blob/main/CODE_REVIEW.md). The self-test that guards the scanner and the delete paths grew from 88 checks to 125.

Scan speed and treemap layout are unchanged: a 427,000-file folder still scans in about 6 seconds, and its treemap still lays out in about 20 milliseconds.
