# Wizzzee 0.6.2

Wizzzee is a disk space analyzer for macOS in the shape of [WizTree](https://diskanalyzer.com/): a folder tree with sizes, a list of the biggest files, and a colored treemap you can click into. It scans a full startup disk — around 3.9 million files — in roughly 15 seconds.

**This release fixes one page of the guide.** Nothing else in the app has changed.

## The guide's page on marking fits the guide

**Help ▸ Welcome to Wizzzee** has seven pages. The sixth, *Mark and Remove*, was drawn wrongly in 0.6.1:

- **It was cut off at both sides.** The list of pages down the left had lost its margin, with the icon and the titles hard against the edge, and the picture and the cards ran to the right-hand edge with none either.
- **It scrolled, and no other page does.** The last of its six cards ran off the foot of the guide.

Both came from the picture at the head of that page. 0.6.1 redrew it with the tabs and the bar for the marks in one row, and that row cannot be squeezed: it was 33 points wider than the page had for it. The page grew to hold it and took the whole guide with it, past its own edges.

- The picture now gives the room at its sides, which is there for the numbered dots beside its rows, to those rows only. The tabs' row has the picture's full width.
- **The guide is a little bigger**: 940 by 700 points, from 900 by 680. That is what holds six cards without scrolling. The other pages have four cards and the same pictures, with a little more room around them.

## Limits

- **In a window at its shortest the guide is taller than the window**, and hangs about 40 points below its foot (it was about 20). All of it is still on screen and its buttons work.
- **`⌘Q` does nothing while the guide is open.** A window with a sheet over it does not quit, and the guide is a sheet. Close the guide first — `Esc` does it — and then quit. This is in 0.6.0 and 0.6.1 as well.

## Installing

Download `Wizzzee-0.6.2-macos-universal.zip` below, unzip it, and move `Wizzzee.app` to Applications, replacing the old one.

- **First launch.** The app is not notarized, so macOS blocks it the first time. Open it once, dismiss the warning, then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**. macOS asks only once.
- **Full Disk Access has to be granted again.** macOS ties that permission to the exact build, so the grant from an earlier version does not carry over. In **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access**, remove the old Wizzzee entry and add the new app. Without it, Mail, Messages, Safari and other users' folders are skipped and the totals read low.

It runs on macOS 14 or newer, on Apple silicon and Intel.

## Internal

A scroll view that scrolls up and down is still at least as wide as what is in it, so one row that could not be squeezed widened its page, and the page widened the guide inside a frame that was not going to give: the surplus was cut off, half at each side. That is why a picture that was too wide showed up as a margin missing from the list at the other side of the guide.

Nothing had checked for it. The self-test asked that the guide came up at its size, and it did. It now lays out all seven pages and holds each one inside the guide, and what is on each inside its page with nothing to scroll to: three more checks, 766 from 763. Run against 0.6.1's layout, two of them fail.
